# My Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

@brand/voice.md

## Folders

- `brand/` — who the business is: voice, tone, logo notes, and words to use or avoid.
- `leads/` — the people or groups we want to reach, described as audiences (sample data only).
- `campaigns/` — one folder or file per campaign: the plan, the emails, and the subject lines.
- `notes/` — research, decisions, and anything else worth remembering.

## Rules

1. **Write in plain English.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon.
2. **Never send email to real people.** Write drafts only. Do not send, schedule, or test-send anything to a real address.
3. **Ask me before deleting anything.** This includes files, folders, and text inside files.
