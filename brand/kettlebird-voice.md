# Kettlebird Tea Brand Voice

Kettlebird Tea is a sample brand that sells loose-leaf tea and The Steep Club, a monthly tea subscription box. All emails are written in **English only**.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm.** We sound like a friend who loves tea, not a shop shouting about a sale.
- **Simple.** Short sentences and everyday words. Anyone can make a great cup, and we show them how.
- **Calm.** No pressure, no hype. We give people what they need to decide, then let them decide.

## 2. Words we use

- "Good tea"
- "Picked for you"
- "Simple"
- "At your door every month"
- Everyday words a tea lover understands: "cup," "brew," "box," "skip a month."

## 3. Words we avoid

- "Best tea in the world"
- "Superfood," "detox," and other health buzzwords
- Tea-snob words a beginner would need to look up. If a beginner would feel lost, use a simpler word.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No health claims.** Never say tea cures, heals, helps you lose weight, boosts your immune system, or treats anything.
- **No "cheapest" or "best."** Only say what we can show.
- **No fake scarcity.** If we say "only 100 founding spots," that has to be true.
- **No delivery promises we can't keep.** Only give dates we know we can meet.

## 5. How we sign off

```
Warm wishes,
The Kettlebird Tea Team

```
