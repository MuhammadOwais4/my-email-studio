# Soft-Technix Brand Voice

Soft-Technix builds websites, custom software, mobile apps and digital marketing for small businesses in Pakistan. All emails are written in **English only**.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Friendly.** We sound like a helpful neighbour, not a big company. Warm, polite, and easy to talk to.
- **Expert.** We know our work and show it through clear advice, not big words.
- **Straight-talking.** We say what we mean, keep it short, and don't oversell.

## 2. Words we use

- "We'll help you grow"
- "Simple"
- "Practical"
- Everyday words a busy shop owner understands: "website," "app," "more customers," "easy to update."

## 3. Words we avoid

- "Cutting-edge"
- "Synergy"
- Other buzzwords and tech jargon. If a small business owner would need to look it up, use a simpler word.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No guaranteed Google rankings.** Never promise "first page on Google" or any ranking.
- **No sales numbers.** Never promise "double your sales" or any growth figure.
- **No lowest prices.** Never say we are the cheapest or have the lowest prices in Pakistan.

## 5. How we sign off

```
Best regards,
The Soft-Technix Team
softtechnix.com
```
