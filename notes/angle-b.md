# Angle B: Discovering new teas

Subject line ideas for Email 1 ("It's here") in `campaigns/steep-club-launch/`.
Brand: Kettlebird Tea. Voice file: `brand/kettlebird-voice.md`.

The idea: every month brings a tea you haven't tried yet, picked for you, so your cup never gets boring.

## Subject lines

1. **A new tea to try, every single month**
2. **Good tea you haven't met yet, picked for you**
3. **The Steep Club is open. What will you try first?**

## Notes

- All three are about trying something new, not about tea being "the best" or doing anything for your health.
- No tea-snob words. A beginner can read each line and know what it means.
- Line 2 uses the brand phrases "good tea" and "picked for you."
- Line 3 is the only one that names the club, like line 3 in Angle A (`notes/angle-a.md`). Good if we want to test the club name against no club name.
- We don't name tea origins yet, because `[where the tea comes from]` is still a placeholder in the plan. Once we know, a line like "Teas from [origin], at your door" could work.
- Pair any of these with the current preview line: "You asked us to tell you when it opened. Here's a thank-you just for the waitlist."
