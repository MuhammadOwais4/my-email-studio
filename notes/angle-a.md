# Angle A: A calm daily ritual

Subject line ideas for Email 1 ("It's here") in `campaigns/steep-club-launch/`.
Brand: Kettlebird Tea. Voice file: `brand/kettlebird-voice.md`.

The idea: the Steep Club gives you a quiet moment with a good cup each day, without having to think about what to buy.

## Subject lines

1. **Your quiet cup each day starts here**
2. **A calm cup, at your door every month**
3. **The Steep Club is open. Time to put the kettle on**

## Notes

- All three talk about the moment with the cup, not what tea does for your body. No health claims ("relaxing," "reduces stress," "helps you sleep").
- No hype, no pressure, no "best." Fits the warm, simple, calm voice.
- Line 2 uses the brand phrase "at your door every month."
- Line 3 is the only one that names the club. Use it if we want people to know what the email is about at a glance.
- Pair any of these with the current preview line: "You asked us to tell you when it opened. Here's a thank-you just for the waitlist."
